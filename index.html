An oversized reading of the running shoe.

The Track takes the layered upper of a trail runner and rebuilds it at an exaggerated scale. Mismatched mesh panels sit under a moulded cage, stacked on a chunky two-tier outsole designed to read from a distance.

Upper

Layered mesh and nylon panels, bonded and stitched, with a moulded TPU cage over the midfoot.

Sole

Two-tier rubber outsole with a chunky lugged tread, built for a heavy, grounded stance.

Fit
